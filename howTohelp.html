What you can do

We can all help save our ocean by making small changes in our everyday lives. We can reduce plastic use, recycle properly, avoid littering, save water, and choose products that are better for the environment. We can also help by keeping beaches and waterways clean and encouraging others to care for marine life. If everyone does their part, we can help protect our oceans for future generations.
