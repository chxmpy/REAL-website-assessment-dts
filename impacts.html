Impacts

On

The

Ocean

Ocean pollution has a major impact on marine life and the health of our oceans. Plastic, chemicals, oil, and other waste can harm or kill sea animals and damage important habitats such as coral reefs. Pollution can also enter the food chain, affecting both wildlife and humans. Reducing waste and keeping our waterways clean is essential for protecting our oceans for future generations.
